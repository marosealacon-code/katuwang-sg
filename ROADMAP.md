## Project rules
- Keep essential rights and safety resources free.
- Use verified official sources for legal and immigration information.
- Mark unverified information clearly.
- Never claim a feature works until tested.
- Prefer free tools until there is a genuine need to upgrade.
- Do not collect sensitive personal information without a justified and secure process.

The project is in the documentation stage. No website has been built, tested, or deployed yet.
