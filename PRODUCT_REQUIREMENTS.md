# Katuwang SG — Product Requirements

## Mission
Create a trusted, mobile-friendly support portal for Singapore domestic helpers and their employers.

## Target users
- Filipino, Indonesian, Myanmar, Indian, and other migrant domestic workers in Singapore.
- Employers of domestic helpers in Singapore.

## Core features
1. Help and safety support directory.
2. Singapore employment rules and official MOM resources.
3. Hiring and onboarding guides.
4. Passport, Work Permit, and overseas travel guides.
5. Medical check-up and insurance information.
6. Free and paid learning resources.
7. Singapore day-off activities.
8. Household chore, childcare, eldercare, and pet-care checklists.
9. Verified service directory.
10. Donations and ethical income opportunities.

## Trust and safety
- Use official sources for legal and immigration information.
- Record source URLs and verification dates.
- Clearly label information awaiting verification.
- Keep emergency contacts and essential rights information free.
- Never invent laws, contacts, fees, or visa requirements.
- Protect user privacy and avoid collecting sensitive information unnecessarily.

## Initial build
- Mobile-first responsive website.
- Employer and Helper entry points.
- Searchable resource directory.
- Help and Support page.
- No user accounts or payment processing in the first phase.

## Sustainability
Explore voluntary donations, sponsored free lessons, optional paid courses, and transparent affiliate partnerships.

## Current status
Planning and repository setup. Website not yet built, tested, or deployed.
