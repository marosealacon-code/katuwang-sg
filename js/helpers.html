For helpers

Simple information and tools to help you feel prepared and know where to go if you need help.

What MOM says

  • Employers must provide enough daily rest and weekly rest days.
  • Employers must also give at least one rest day a month that cannot be exchanged for money.
  • If you need help, you can call the MOM MDW Helpline on 1800-339-5505 or the CDE on 1800-2255-233.

Salary

MOM’s salary guide says employers should pay salary on time and in full, should not keep your salary even if you ask them to, and lists deductions that are not allowed.

Practical tips

General tips, not legal advice.

  • Save important numbers in your phone before you need them.
  • Keep your own simple record of salary received, with the date.
  • If something is unclear, ask politely and ask for it to be explained slowly.
  • If you feel unsafe, stop and contact help. See Help & Support.
