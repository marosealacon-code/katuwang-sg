Respectful routines

  • Write down tasks and routines together, and agree on them.
  • Explain things slowly and in simple words. Check understanding kindly.
  • Give feedback privately and calmly. Say what you need, not just what went wrong.
  • Plan rest days ahead so that care for children or family is covered.
  • Pay salary on time and keep a record.

General tips, not legal advice.
