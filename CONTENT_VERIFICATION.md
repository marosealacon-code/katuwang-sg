# Katuwang SG — Content Verification Standards

## 1. Our Commitment
Katuwang SG aims to provide clear, practical, trustworthy information for migrant domestic workers and employers in Singapore.

Accuracy, safety, dignity, and transparency come before speed or monetisation.

## 2. Preferred Sources
Use authoritative sources appropriate to the topic:
- Singapore Ministry of Manpower (MOM): Work Permits, employment rules, and domestic worker guidance.
- Singapore government websites: official processes, services, and requirements.
- Embassies and consulates: passport renewals and nationality-specific services.
- Immigration authorities of the destination country: visa and entry requirements.
- Healthcare providers and relevant authorities: medical examination requirements.
- Official insurers and policy documents: insurance benefits, exclusions, and terms.

Use other sources only when appropriate, and clearly distinguish them from official guidance.

## 3. Required Record for Each Important Guide
Record:
- Page title and topic
- Intended audience and relevant nationality, if applicable
- Original source name
- Direct source URL
- Date checked
- Last reviewed date
- Content status: Draft, Needs Review, Verified, or Outdated
- Important limitations or unresolved questions

## 4. Verification Rules
- Never invent laws, fees, deadlines, contact details, eligibility rules, or visa requirements.
- Read the original source before marking information Verified.
- Confirm that the source applies to the correct nationality and situation.
- Check whether requirements vary by date, employer, destination, or individual circumstances.
- Link directly to the relevant official page where possible.
- If information cannot be confirmed, label it Needs Review and explain what remains uncertain.
- Review time-sensitive information regularly and after major official announcements.
- If a source changes or a guide becomes unreliable, mark it Outdated until checked again.
- Record the date the information was last verified; do not imply that old information is current.

## 5. Legal, Medical, and Insurance Limits
Katuwang SG provides general information and educational resources, not personalised legal, medical, immigration, or insurance advice.

Encourage users to contact the appropriate official authority or qualified professional for case-specific questions.

Insurance comparisons must explain the basis of comparison, policy date, exclusions, limits, and relevant terms. Do not guarantee coverage or recommend a policy solely because it offers a commission.

## 6. Safety and Emergency Information
- Keep essential rights and safety information freely accessible.
- Verify emergency and support contact details against reliable official sources before publication.
- Explain the scope and limitations of each support service.
- Katuwang SG is an information portal, not an emergency-response service.
- Do not invite users to post passport numbers, Work Permit details, medical records, home addresses, or other sensitive personal information publicly.
- Do not publish allegations or identifying details about workers or employers without appropriate safeguards and review.

## 7. Languages and Accessibility
- Use plain, respectful English.
- Avoid blaming, shaming, or discriminatory language.
- When translating guidance, preserve the original meaning and clearly flag translations that have not been reviewed.
- Make important safety information easy to find on mobile devices.
- Do not claim that a translation is official or professionally reviewed unless that is true.

## 8. Transparency and Corrections
- Provide a way for users to report outdated or incorrect information without requiring them to disclose sensitive details.
- Correct errors promptly and record significant changes.
- Clearly disclose sponsorships, affiliate links, and paid placements.
- Never let a commercial relationship override safety or factual accuracy.
- Do not claim charitable status, nonprofit status, or tax-deductible donations unless independently confirmed.

## 9. Publication Checklist
Before publishing an important guide, confirm:
- [ ] The source has been opened and checked.
- [ ] The URL works and supports the relevant claim.
- [ ] Nationality and situation-specific requirements are clear.
- [ ] The verification and review dates are recorded.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Personal data is not unnecessarily collected.
- [ ] The language is respectful and understandable.
- [ ] Any commercial relationship is disclosed.

## 10. Current Project Status
This document establishes the intended standards. It does not mean that any guide has been researched, verified, or published.

Current status: planning and repository documentation only. The website has not yet been built, tested, or deployed.
